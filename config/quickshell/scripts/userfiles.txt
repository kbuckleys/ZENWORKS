# THE FILES IN THE SUITE'S CONFIG DIRECTORIES THAT ARE THIS MACHINE'S, not
# the package's. One list, read by sync-package.sh (kept out of the
# package), update.sh (never laid over) and the package's uninstall (saved,
# or removed when they are only generated). Paths are relative to
# $XDG_CONFIG_HOME; * matches within a name.
#
#   generic    the package ships a stock copy; this machine's own choice
#              lives here and is never shipped, updated over or lost
#   generated  written here from a choice made elsewhere (the theme, the
#              fonts) by ThemeSync and fontsync.sh on every start; never
#              shipped (absent is the default) and safe to remove
#   saved      made here by the user; never shipped, always kept
#   seed       shipped as they are on the maintainer's machine, a starting
#              point; after that the user's: never updated over, and kept
#              by uninstall when they differ from the package's
